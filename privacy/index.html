Privacy Policy

Application: Manut8

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Purpose

Manut8 is a private utility used to synchronize files from a personal homelab environment to Google Drive using rclone.

Google Drive access

The application requests access to Google Drive only to perform file synchronization explicitly configured by the owner of the homelab. Google OAuth is used to authenticate the account and authorize the requested Drive access.

Data handling

Files and Google Drive data accessed by the application are used only for the configured synchronization process. The application does not sell personal information, use Drive data for advertising, or share Drive data with third parties.

Credentials

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally on the homelab and are not intentionally transmitted to third parties, except to Google services required for authentication and Google Drive API access.

Contact

This is a personal homelab utility and does not provide a public support service.
